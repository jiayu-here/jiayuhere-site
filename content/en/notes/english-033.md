## Key Points
**Concept**: Usage of `a`
- `a` + a noun `->` indicates general reference;

**Question Type**: Central idea supplement - excluding distracting options
- Features of interference options:
- (1) One-sided;
- (2) Write the essay backward;
	- Suppose you hesitate between options B and D. You can use options B and D as article titles and create your own essay to see if the logic of the article is roughly consistent with the original;

**Concept**: `only` placed at the beginning of a sentence, it can be inverted
- Advance the auxiliary verb;

## Reading Summary
- Observe the author's adjectives in the article to judge their attitude and tendencies;
- Detail questions: The simple method is to test **word substitution and semantic understanding**, but the more often in-depth tests are the substitution of **logical concepts**;
- While reading, `in that` keep up with the cause;
- Reasoning true/false questions: First, **find the center**, then **find the turning point**;
- If the option contains `some`, it indicates a **softened tone** option and is more likely to be the correct answer;
- Add `" "` to nouns in the article, which may express **opposite meanings** and can be translated as "so-called";
