```Text
Dear Tom,

I am delighted to hear that you intend to look for a job in China. As your friend, I would like to offer several suggestions.

To begin with, I suggest looking for work in Beijing because the capital offers a wide range of employment opportunities. You might consider applying for an English-teaching position at a language school or training organization. As a native English speaker with part-time teaching experience, you would have relevant strengths for such a role.

I believe that your teaching could help Chinese students learn effectively, become more enthusiastic about English, and improve their spoken fluency. If you would like any additional information or assistance, please feel free to contact me.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
```
