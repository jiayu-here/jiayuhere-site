## 1.1 Advanced Mathematics
**Outline**
- Differential Calculus of Single-variable Functions (Calculus in one variable is the**foundation, key point, and difficulty**)
	- Calculus
		- Functions, limits (**Difficulty**), continuity
		- Derivatives and differentials
		- Mean value theorem for differential (**Difficulty**) and the application of derivatives
	- Integral Calculus
		- Indefinite integral
		- Definite integrals (**Difficulty**) and improper integrals
		- Application of Definite Integrals (**Key Point**)
- Multivariable Differential Calculus
	- Calculus
		- Functions, limits (heavy limits, but not tested), continuous
		- Partial Derivatives and Total Differentials (Focuses on Total Derivatives)
		- Applications of differential calculus (Extrema and Extrema, Multivariate Has No Differential Mean Value Theorem)
	- Integral Calculus
		- Multiple integrals (double integral (**difficulty**) and triple integral)
		- Linear area division
		- Points application
- Spatial analytic geometry and vector algebra
- Differential equations
	- First-order equation
	- Reduced-order equation
	- Higher-order linear equations
- Infinite series
	- Series of Constant Terms (**Difficulty**)
	- Power Series (**Difficulty**)
	- Fourier series
