```Text
In the drawing, several young people are exercising in a park with smiles on their faces. One of them comments, "This newly built neighborhood park is wonderful." The accompanying chart shows that the number of parks rose sharply from 406 in 2020 to 670 in 2022.

The scene reflects the increasingly important role of public parks in daily life. China's economic development has enabled local governments to devote more resources to public spaces and community facilities.

Parks provide residents with convenient places to relax and exercise, thereby supporting both physical and mental health. In my own case, exercising in a nearby park for half an hour each morning helps me remain energetic and concentrate more effectively on my studies.

Governments should continue developing accessible public parks while considering the needs of different communities. Citizens, in turn, should use these spaces responsibly and help protect their natural environment.
```
