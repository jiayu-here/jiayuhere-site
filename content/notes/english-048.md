**模板**
```Text
										Notice

			                                                                   Friday, June 21
    It is particularly delightful to hear that Prof. Smith is working on a research project about campus sports activities. This notice is intended to recruit one student as a team member.

    To begin with, it is advisable for the applicant to possess relevant ability and experience in organizing sports activities. Furthermore, the candidate is supposed to have ample knowledge and skills for carrying out academic study, such as collecting and analyzing data and writing reports or papers; these skills will help ensure the success of the project. Finally, it is necessary for the applicant to communicate and cooperate effectively with other members.

    The interview is scheduled to start at 19:00 on June 25 in the office of Prof. Smith and welcome you all to this interview.

                                                                              Li Ming.
```
