**模板**
```Text
	This is a simple but enlightening drawing, in which there is a lovely and cute boy with a little disappointment on his face, complaining, "I want to learn traditional opera, but many of my classmates feel that it is boring." Then his father encourages him: "It is significant for you to be keen on it."

    Such a scene, and the father's encouragement, can be associated with the significance of sticking to one's dream. From my point of view, successful individuals are characterized by excellent qualities such as confidence, optimism, and independent thinking. However, in reality, many youngsters tend to be influenced by others' opinions and even give up what they really want to pursue.

    This phenomenon deserves particular attention because these young people care too much about others' comments, ignoring the importance of persisting in their own thoughts. Unfortunately, this negative state of mind can become an obstacle to their long-term growth and progress.

    As a college student, I deem it advisable for young people to do what they like, which can give them strength, inspire their passion for striving, and help them achieve their dreams. Meanwhile, everyone should bear in mind that it is tremendous effort, rather than others' comments, that enables us to embrace success and a promising future. After all, interest is the best teacher.
```
