**模板**
```Text
Dear Tom,

    As your friend, I'm quite pleased to hear that you intend to find a job in China, and today I am writing to make several suggestions.

   To begin with, from my point of view, it is advisable for you to look for a job in Beijing because the capital offers many employment opportunities. In addition, you are supposed to apply for the position of English teacher at a training organization. Compared with others, you have many advantages as a native English speaker and have worked as a part-time English teacher.

    Finally, I believe that your excellent teaching will help Chinese students learn efficiently, inspire their passion for learning, and help them speak English fluently. If you have any other ideas or requirements, please feel free to inform me.

                                                                   Your sincerely,
                                                                            Li Ming.
```
