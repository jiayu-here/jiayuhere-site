3.1 Modality of Finite Verbs

3.1.1 What is verb modality?

What is Modality?

  • Modality expresses possibility, necessity, permission, ability, willingness, prediction, or the speaker's attitude;
  • Core pattern: modal verb + base form of the lexical verb;

Common Modal Verbs

3.1.2 How to Use Modal Verbs

Common Features

  • Features:
  • 1. A modal is followed by the base form of the lexical verb;
  • 2. Do not use am/is/are after a modal; use the base form be when needed;
  • 3. The tense change of modal verbs is limited;
  • Common verbs have multiple tenses;
  • Modal verbs generally have only two tenses: present and past;
  • 4. Modal verbs do not change with person or number
  • No third-person verb inflection
  • 5. Modal verbs become negative or questioning (frequently tested)
  • Negative: add not after the modal;
  • Question: place the modal before the subject;
  • Give an example
  • i can do something and i could do something

Usage

  • Words: could, would, should, might
  • Meaning 1: Indicates the past;
  • Meaning 2: Indicates the present, but in a more euphemistic tone or polite tone;
  • can you help me and could you help me
  • Meaning 3: Used in subjunctive mood

3.1.3 Usage 1: What kind of emotional attitude to express

Usage 1: must

  • Meaning:
  • Strong emotional attitude
  • Must
  • Example:
  • we must also act with caution.
  • We must also act cautiously;

Usage 2: can/could

  • Meaning
  • 1. Being able to do something;
  • 2. Being able to do something, indicating permission;
  • Give an example
  • Could a hug a day keep the doctor away?
  • Can a hug a day keep doctors away from me?

Usage 3: will/would

  • Meaning
  • 1. In the future tense, it indicates that it will be;
  • 2. Expressing a willingness;
  • Example:
  • Will you marry me?
  • Would you marry me?
  • The first draft will appear on the page...

Usage 4: May/Might

  • Meaning
  • 1. Permission;
  • 2. Possibility;
  • Give an example
  • expressions may influence emotion rather than just the other way around.

Usage 5: should

  • Meaning
  • Indicates that it should be;
  • Give an example
  • Those suffering from persistent nightmares should seek help from a therapist.

3.1.4 Usage 2: Indicates speculation or possibility

Modal verbs can express different degrees of certainty

Modal verb + do

  • Distinction:
  • must: strong logical conclusion;
  • may, might, could: possibility;
  • should: expectation;
  • can't/cannot: strong conclusion that something is impossible or untrue;
  • Give an example
  • The details may be unknowable...
  • These details may be unknown;
  • may be is a modal verb plus the base form be;
  • maybe is a single adverb meaning “perhaps”;
  • It might be poetic, philosophical, sensual, or mathematical, but in any case it must… have something to do with the soul of the human being.
  • Two modal verbs that are speculated but have different possibilities;

Modal Verbs and Speculation

  • Present-state inference: modal + base form;
  • Inference about an action in progress: modal + be doing;
  • Now something similar could be happening in the oceans.
  • Now some similar things are happening in the ocean;
  • Inference about the past: modal + have done
  • must have done expresses a strong conclusion about a past event;
  • She must have cried. / She must be crying.

Additional Modal Verb Phrases

  • have to: must, have to do it;
  • They have to be careful not to come across as disappointed in their child
  • They must be careful not to show disappointment on the child's face;
  • ought to: also indicates that something should be done;

3.2 Past-Paper Practice

Question:Online education can supplement the traditional kind

  • Online education can supplement traditional education;

Question:He must store a large quantity of grain instead of consuming all his grain immediately

  • must: must
  • He must store large amounts of grain rather than consume it;

Question:This could add value to their businesses in three ways

  • could expresses a possible benefit;

Title:Upcoming reforms might bring the price to a more reasonable level

  • Maybe, can;
