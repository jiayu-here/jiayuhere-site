Key Points

Concept: -ness suffix

  • Concept:
  • Added after adjectives to form abstract nouns, indicating nature, situation, or state;
  • Example:
  • decisiveness: decisive
  • greatness: Great
  • goodness: Good deeds
  • badness: Bad deeds
  • weakness: Weak
  • darkness: Darkness
  • willingness: Willingly
  • commonness: ordinary

Concept: Attributive clauses are often a key focus in exams

-

Reading Summary

  • In the it seems ... of the article, seem often follows viewpoints the author disagrees with;
  • Pronoun reference principle: proximate reference;
  • When asking about characteristics or characteristics, find adjectives in the article <- AG：The importance of education in poor countries ...;
