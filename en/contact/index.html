Before contacting

Three details prevent unnecessary back-and-forth.

It does not need to be long. A clear goal, environment and evidence make it easier to begin with the real problem.

  1. 01

    Goal

    Describe what you want to achieve and the expected input, output or final behavior.

  2. 02

    Environment

    List the relevant hardware, software, tool versions and steps you have already tried.

  3. 03

    Evidence

    Attach a minimal reproduction, error, log, waveform or screenshot and mark the unexpected part.
